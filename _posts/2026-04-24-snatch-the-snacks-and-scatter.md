*"Snatch the Snacks and Scatter"*
*(a parody of Steve Miller Band's "Take the Money and Run")*

**Verse 1**

This here's a story 'bout a tuxedo named Mars,
A black-and-white outlaw with her eye on the jars.
She'll purr at your ankles, she'll meow at the door —
Then the second you blink, she's halfway 'cross the floor.

**Chorus**

Go on, snatch the snacks and scat!
Snatch the snacks and scatter, yeah.
Go on, snatch the snacks and scat!
Snatch the snacks and scatter, yeah.

**Chorus (repeat)**

Go on, snatch the snacks and scat!
Snatch the snacks and scatter, yeah.
Go on, snatch the snacks and scat!
Snatch the snacks and scatter, yeah.

**Verse 2**

Mars saw a chicken strip on the edge of the plate.
She crouched in the shadows, oh, she knew how to wait.
One paw, one pounce, and the whole thing was gone —
Down the hall, past the kitchen, and behind the chaise lounge.

**Chorus**

Go on, snatch the snacks and scat!
Snatch the snacks and scatter, yeah.
Go on, snatch the snacks and scat!
Snatch the snacks and scatter, yeah.

**Chorus (repeat)**

Go on, snatch the snacks and scat!
Snatch the snacks and scatter, yeah.
Go on, snatch the snacks and scat!
Snatch the snacks and scatter, yeah.

*(instrumental break — imagine a guitar solo while Mars licks butter off a knife)*

**Verse 3**

Now I'm chasin' that cat with a dish towel in hand.
She's a slippery little bandit, won't you please understand?
She'll cry that she's starving, she'll roll on her back,
But the moment you turn 'round, she's planning her attack.

**Chorus**

Go on, snatch the snacks and scat!
Snatch the snacks and scatter, yeah.
Go on, snatch the snacks and scat!
Snatch the snacks and scatter, yeah.

**Chorus (repeat)**

Go on, snatch the snacks and scat!
Snatch the snacks and scatter, yeah.
Go on, snatch the snacks and scat!
Snatch the snacks and scatter, yeah.

**Outro**

Mars, Mars, where'd you go, Mars?
Under the couch in her little white spats.
Mars, Mars, you sneaky little star —
Snatch the snacks and scatter, that's just who you are.

Mars, Mars, where'd you go, Mars?
Hidin' in the closet with a mouthful of cheese.
Mars, Mars, you sneaky little star —
Snatch the snacks and scatter, if you please.

*Now that matches the original's pattern more faithfully — three verses with doubled choruses between them, an instrumental break, and a tagged outro with two turns of the refrain.*
