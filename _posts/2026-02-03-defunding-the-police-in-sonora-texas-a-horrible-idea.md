<p>
    If people in Austin, Texas tried to disband the Austin Police Department, Greg Abbott would be threatening to
    take over with state troopers. Oh wait, that happened not long ago.  It was a controversy made into a circus
    by the media and politicians. But if the same thing happens in a small west Texas town, does it matter?
</p>
<p>
    No.  Not if it's done by pseudo-conservatives bent on snatching defeat from the jaws of victory as they have for
    over a century in Sonora, Texas. Here in the People's Republic of Sonorastan, we believe progress is best served
    by preventing it at all costs.
</p>
<p>
    With I-27 on the horizon, you'd think Sonora would be looking at a bright future.  Instead, they're looking at
    a future of stagnation and decay.  Why change?  We don't like change.  We're happy with the status quo...poverty
    and an ability to blame the lefties for failure.
</p>
<p>
    It's a sad state of affairs, but that's the way it is in Sonora, Texas.  We're stuck in a cycle of decline and
    decay, and it's all because of the people who run this town (not those elected, but the 50% of this down that 
    actually vote and the other 50% who are too damned lazy to give a damn what happens in their community, beyond
    their own self-interest and complaining on Facebook).
</p>
<p>
    When I was a kid, this down was FOUR TIMES the size it is today.  It had leaders like Leroy Valliant and Jimmy
    Cahill trying to turn around the sinking ship of an oil-and-gas dependent economy.  They sat in the Fiddler 
    restaurant and talked about how we didn't just need to buy local, we needed to bring in more business. It was,
    I now admit, a fool's errand to talk like that in a town that loses the brightest and most talented of its
    population each year when the kids graduate from high school and do the smart thing...LEAVE!
</p>
<p>
    Our police department does it's job.  I see them stopping traffic on a regular basis on my security cameras.
    They are involved in our school safety.  They are out there constantly patrolling in a town that supports them
    about as much as the rotting struts of some of the houses here support the collapsing roofs.  It's a town, like
    those roofs with a good group of professional police officers supported by a town that is rotted to its core.
</p>
<p>
    I doubt writing this will sway any opinions.  I'm increasingly convinced that most of this town can't read more
    than three sentences without losing their attention span.  Nonetheless, it will be a tragic mistake if this town
    does something so dumb as to defund the police department in Sonora, Texas.  I know, we're using language like
    "disband," but let's call this what it is...defunding.  Let's admit our hypocrisy loud and proud and admit that
    there are three types of people here: Those smart enough to leave, those too dumb to leave and the dumbest among
    us (like me) who return to this shit-hole town thinking we can change any of it.
</p>
